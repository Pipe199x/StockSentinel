Questions

Is this investment advice?+

No. The picks show what StockSentinel's rules select. They are not a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and they don't consider your situation.

Are the results real money?+

No. The past test and the model portfolio are hypothetical: they apply the rules to market data, including trading costs. Past results don't promise future returns.

How often are there new picks?+

The site updates every market day. Some days have several picks, some have none, and in a market stress period StockSentinel does not trade.

Where do I use my license key?+

On trading.oceandatagroup.ai: press Unlock and paste it. The site remembers it on that browser for 7 days.
