✦ Machine Learning · XGBoost
How the model works

Every day after the NYSE close, an XGBoost model (400 trees, depth 4) is retrained per stock on prices since 2020. It predicts the next-day return, which is then converted to a price. Features:

1-day return5-day return5-day SMA20-day SMA RSI 1410-day avg. volumePrevious closePrevious volumeSame-day close & volume

“Direction accuracy” and “live error” compare every published prediction with the close that actually happened afterwards, so they are out-of-sample. “Backtest error” is measured on the last 30 days of the training data itself and tends to be optimistic.
